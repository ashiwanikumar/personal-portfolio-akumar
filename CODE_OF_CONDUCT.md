# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to maintaining a respectful, professional, and harassment-free environment for everyone who contributes to or interacts with this project.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be clear, practical, and collaborative when reporting issues or suggesting changes.
- Respect privacy, credentials, internal data, and project confidentiality.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information, secrets, credentials, or sensitive project data.
- Deliberate disruption of development, review, deployment, or support work.
- Abusive, hostile, or repeated off-topic communication.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, restrict access, or take other appropriate action when this code of conduct is violated.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to this repository, related project communication, issue tracking, pull requests, reviews, and deployment/support collaboration.
